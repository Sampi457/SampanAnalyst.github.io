Datasets-
>CovidDeaths: Daily COVID-19 cases, deaths, and population by country.
>CovidVacc: Daily vaccination counts and country-level socioeconomic data including GDP and life expectancy.

Objectives-
>Examine how COVID-19 cases and deaths vary across countries and continents.
>Analyze death rates in relation to population size and socioeconomic indicators.
>Investigate vaccination coverage across countries and its relationship with population size.

Methodology-

Data Cleaning & Preparation:
>Filtered incomplete or null entries (e.g., missing death counts or population data).
>Extracted key variables for analysis, including total cases, total deaths, population, GDP, and life expectancy.
>Calculated derived metrics such as death percentage, infection percentage, and vaccination coverage.

Exploratory Analysis & Aggregation:
>Identified countries and continents with the highest infection and death counts.
>Computed global totals and percentages over time.
>Examined relationships between socioeconomic factors (GDP, life expectancy) and COVID-19 deaths.
> Performed regression analysis in R to examine the predictive relationship between socioeconomic factors and COVID-19 death rates, identifying patterns and trends across countries.
> Visualization & communication: Created clear, data-driven visualizations using ggplot2 to highlight findings and make insights accessible to a broader audience, supporting evidence-based interpretation of the results.
